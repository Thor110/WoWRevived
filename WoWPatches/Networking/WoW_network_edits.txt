Address notes for the patch DLLs (Smackw32.dll / _inmm.dll)

None of the retail (vanilla) addresses apply to this build. For example 0x405470, which Smackw32 patches to RET in
the retail build, is the middle of an unrelated function here, and 0x4D255C / 0x4D1490 are code, not data.
Both DLLs now identify the build from the CD Player menu constructor prologue (WoWPatches/Common/GameCommon.h)
and only patch addresses whose bytes match.
