Summary:

The Martian invasion has stalled. The Martians have inflicted heavy losses on the massed human armies but they have succeeded in bringing the Martian advance to a halt. It is now time to bring war to the enemy.

The date is 1907

The heroic efforts on the part of the Human defenders have proved decisive. The GHQ defensive line has held exterminating hundreds of Martian fighting machines. Their wreckage lies strewn across the midlands and much is being learned from the analysis of their devices. Our scientists have devised new technologies which are now coming into play. These technologies will prove decisive in the forthcoming assault on the Martian-held territories.

Our armies have pushed the Martians back to the borders of Scotland but in the process we have lost the bulk of our men and materiel. The fighting has been hard but the Martians have borne the brunt of it. What remains of our victorious armies are now thinly stretched in an attempt to hold onto the territories we have so dearly won. Our spies have indicated that the Martians are about to undertake a massive reorganisation and redeployment of their forces. It is believed they are bringing new types of fighting machine into the fray, machines that we have not yet encountered and which we believe will be a difficult proposition to defeat. 

This reorganisation will allow us a short breathing space until the Martians are ready to redouble their assault. Our men have the confidence to take on the enemy for the first time in the knowledge that we can win. Our Generals are not so sure. Thanks to our spies we have prior knowledge of the Martian plans but what we know does not instil us with confidence. We expect to have to trade our recently reconquered territories for time whilst we rebuild our shattered but triumphant forces and re-equip them with the newest and best weapons in our arsenal.

The choices for us are not difficult, we know it will take a large proportion of our exisiting resources to attempt to hold the next Martian counter attack. As long as we succeed then the future is assured. We believe that our technologies will rival the new martian weaponry and perhaps master them. Our numbers have already proven to be decisive and with massive new armies we are set to succeed.

We know that if the Martians are left to themselves they will amass a new and more deadly army and ultimately they will attack us with irresistible force. Defence is not an option, only attack can bring us victory.

Difficulty:

For the humans, this scenario will be a challenge due to the strength of the Martian military. This scenario will have it's surprises but ultimately you should succeed in defeating the Martians. Maintain your defences and use them to deter an attack on your territories. When you attack be prepared for heavy losses.

For the Martians, this scenario is fairly easy due to the poor military situation for the Human forces. Their recent advances have left them drained and unable to resist. What they currently lack in miltary forces they make up for in productive capability so an attack on their resource-producing and construction centres is essential. Breaching the GHQ line will prove difficult but it is only a matter of time. The Humans are beaten but they have already shown that they will fight tooth and nail for every sector they defend. The Martian losses will be heavy but they are committed and have no choice but to push on. For the Martians there is no turning back.

Dean Beedell 2005
