Summary: 

The human defences have been successful in checking the Martian invasion. The humans have taken the attack to the enemy.

The date is 1900

The humans have set up a defensive line North of Liverpool from the Lake District stretching across the Pennines to the Wolds. They have successfully fortified this area and have managed to check the Martian invasion with very little difficulty.

The Martians have made no attempt to attack the defensive wall but have attempted to build up their forces and improve their technologies until they have sufficient strength to roll back the massed humans. That was their plan.

This strategy has proven to be a mistake. The humans have used this time to build up their own forces until they have been able to overwhelm anything the Martians have put up against them. The Martian technological superiority has been negated and proven to be nothing in comparison to raw human courage and strength.

The Humans have used their naval vessels to launch a series of combined assaults on the East Coast but as a result there is very little left in reserve. The Royal Navy has been able to launch long range raids against the Martian held areas but has suffered heavy losses. Frantic efforts are being made to fortify those areas recently taken from the Martians. 

Difficulty:

As the humans this scenario is easy. From this position you should be able to walk through the Martians. You have the offensive. If you lose this scenario, well...

As the martians this position is untenable and so if you lose don't be surprised.

Dean Beedell 2004
