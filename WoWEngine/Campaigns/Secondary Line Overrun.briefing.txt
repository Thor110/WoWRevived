This is an Edison recording of John.S.Mitchell, War Correspondant of the London Times speaking from the officer's mess of HMS Invincible.


Summary: 

The northern counties have been successfully assaulted by the Martians and completely overrun. Liverpool, the bastion of defence has finally fallen. The remnant of our defences lie stretched across our secondary defensive positions and scattered in the undefended countryside beyond.

The date is 1905

Despite heroic efforts by our forces to reinforce and defend Liverpool the GHQ line has been overrun. Our primitive machines have turned out to be no match for the superior Martian technologies and numbers. Their war machines have marched across the GHQ line and burnt our defences to the ground. Our strategy to hold the Martian invasion has failed, it has only succeeded in delaying it a little longer.

The Martian strategy appears to be a concentrated advance to the heart and capital of England, London itself but whilst our defences concentrated on this threat Liverpool was lost.

Despite some recent technical advances our technological achievements have shown to be paltry in comparison to those of the Martians. They are certainly currently insufficient to cope with the threat we face. However, some of our  technological progress has been correctly directed toward an effective offensive capability that we hope will also be useful in defence. 

The heroic defence of the capital has lead to some difficult choices for our forces, it has shown that defence however strong cannot contain the Martians by itself but that it must be combined with successful offensive operations. A new direction is certainly required if the course of this war is to be changed. There is some good news. We have received news from our subterranean spies in the Martian held areas that the Martians are now heavily depleted in numbers despite their recent successes. Perhaps now is the time for the assault.

It is not to be doubted that if the Martians are left to themselves they will amass a huge army and then assault our vulnerable, unfortified areas to the West of the defensive chain. The key to the defence of this Kingdom is now the regaining of Liverpool and then the assault on the Eastern coastal counties North of London.

Difficulty:

For the people of this Island, this scenario is a very bleak one indeed. We must rebuild our defences after every assault using our defences to whittle down his numbers. We must take the offensive successfully to the enemy, establishing forward offensive positions and then regain the strategic offensive taking the advantage away from the enemy. 

The long Martian assault has left their lines of supply vulnerable to attack but the Martians know this and they will do everything they can to improve the chances of their next assault succeeding. We have little in the way of resource but we must keep the factories running. The Martians know this and they know they have us beaten but can we turn the situation around?

I somehow doubt we will succeed but it must be done. 

Dean Beedell 2004
