Summary:

The human defences are stretched across the northern counties. They have been successful in checking the Martian invasion.

The date is 1899

The humans have set up a defensive line North of Liverpool in the Lake District stretching across the Pennines to the Wolds. They have attempted to fortify sufficiently to check the Martian invasion. The Martians have made no attempt to attack this defensive wall but are content to build their forces and improve their technologies until they have sufficient strength to roll back the human forces.

Difficulty:

For the humans this scenario is not a difficult one. From this position you should be able to improve your defences and then to take the offensive.

For the Martians this scenario is difficult to win. It is possible to set up killing grounds where you can take out any forces that are thrown against you. It is equally difficult for you to take the offensive against the humans, their defences are incredibly strong and you will need to attack these time and time again before you will be successful.


Dean Beedell 2004
