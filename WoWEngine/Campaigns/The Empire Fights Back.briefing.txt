Summary: 

The British Isles have fallen to the Martian invasion. London has been occupied and the Houses of Parliament have been burnt to the ground. Despite a gallant defence the British Mainland forces have been totally destroyed. The remaining population has either stayed put and suffered under the dominion of the Martians or fled to the coast and hence to the continent. 

The date is 1905

Despite the fall of the British Isles, the British Empire is still in being having not been subject to same level of concerted attack as the mainland. As the Martian forces approached London the Parliamentary members of the British Government have fled en masse to Belgium and have now set up control of the remaining British naval forces from the city of Antwerp. The government in exile found itself in control of significant British Imperial naval and ground forces from India, Africa, Australia and Canada. These forces have been gathered by the Royal Navy to the European mainland reinforced by elements of the Continental armies and navies of France and Germany, who themselves are girdling their armies for the next stage of the Martian invasion. Both Europe and the British Empire have forgotten their internal problems and have pulled themselves together ready to commit itself to the battle. Rather than wait for the continental invasion to begin the Imperial armies have determined to forestall any invasion by attempting a liberation of the English home counties and Eastern coastal regions. The Imperial armies have not wasted their time. They are bringing to battle the very best weapons that man can create. The continental foundries of Krupp and Schneider have poured out weaponry of the highest quality and have re-armed the Imperial forces to a high degree of effectiveness.

The human forces have launched two invasions of the British mainland. The first in the South of England near the town of Deal in Kent has been very successful. The second launched from the Hook of Holland to a portion of the Eastern coast near Grimbsby has gained a foothold but has not succeeded in pushing far inland. The liberation forces in the South have pushed Westward to Cornwall and have also been successful in infiltrating and liberating London. The government of Britain is aware of it's obligations and has transferred it's command back to London as a symbol of defiance and also in order to better control the Imperial forces. construction and defensive structures have been transported from the continent and positioned to exploit and defend the liberated areas. The Imperial armies have landed significant reinforcement forces on all the human held areas and are now preparing to exploit the Martian unpreparedness by pushing our forces deeper inland.

There is a choice for the imperial forces, to push northward to join with the other Imperial forces defending the Eastern coastal regions or push North-Westward to alleviate the critical factor for human effectiveness which is the paucity of resources in the human-held areas. The Imperial forces must exploit every county that is liberated to it's utmost in order to restore the factories to a reasonable level of efficiency. Without these resources the liberation of the British Isles will founder and the Martians will succeed.

There is however, one truth that must be faced, if London falls then the government falls with it. It has sworn to remain in London and fight to the death in the hope of stimulating worldwide resistance to the Martian invasion. Losing London and it's Imperial government command/control structures will result in the flight of all human forces before the relentless Martian war machine.

Difficulty:

As the humans this scenario is extremely difficult. You must NOT on any account let the Martians amass any army capable of assaulting London. As soon as any assault is launched on London the war is over. You MUST take the assault to the Martians and prevent them from ever launching this attack. you must exploit every resource to the maximum and suffer the low output of your factories until you control sufficient resources to start efficient production.

As the Martians you should not be able to lose but be doubly prepared as the human defences are significant and you will have a surprisingly hard time overcoming them.


Note: Turn off all the game display options except the 16-bit display in order to save memory when the Martians attack. Ensure the Companion's autosave function is set to ON whilst this scenario is running.
