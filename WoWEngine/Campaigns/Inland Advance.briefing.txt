Summary: 

The human defences have been successful in checking the Martian invasion. The humans have taken the attack to the enemy.

The date is 1899

The humans have set up a defensive line North of Liverpool from the Lake District stretching across the Pennines to the Wolds. They have successfully fortified this area and have managed to check the Martian invasion. 

The Martians made no attempt to attack this defensive wall but have attempted to build their forces and improve their technologies until they have sufficient strength to roll back the human forces. 

This strategy was a mistake. The humans have used this time to build up their own forces until they have been able to overwhelm anything the Martians have put up against them. The Martian technological superiority has proven to be nothing in comparison to human courage and strength. The Humans have launched a series of ground assaults with all available forces and they have been successful. However, there is now nothing left in reserve. The lines of supply are stretched and vulnerable to attack. The Royal Navy is holding the coasts. Frantic efforts are being made to fortify the areas recently taken from the Martians but there are not enough sappers to build the required defences.

The martians are in no fit state to counter attack and are reeling from the hammer blows dealt to them by the humans.

Difficulty:

This scenario is easy. From this position the humans should be able to walk through the Martians. You have the offensive. 

As the Martians this game is impossible to win.

Dean Beedell 2004
