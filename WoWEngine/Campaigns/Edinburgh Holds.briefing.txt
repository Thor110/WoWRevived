Summary:

The majority of Scotland has fallen swiftly to the Martian Invaders. The northern counties of England have followed in their turn. Even Liverpool has been assaulted by the Martians and rapidly overrun. the Martians are pushing on to the capital of Britain and the Empire, London, in the hope that human resistance will crumble. The only piece of good news for humanity is that the Scottish capital, Edinburgh has held out despite numerous Martian assaults. 

The date is 1902

Despite heroic efforts on the part of the Human defenders the GHQ defensive line has been breached. The Martians are now pouring through in significant numbers. No doubt an attempt will now be made to trap our forces behind the remnants of the GHQ line whilst a frontal assault on our capital is made. In general our weapons have turned out to be no match for those of the Martians unless they they have been used wisely and in large numbers. Where this has been done we have managed to stall the Martian offensive even though we have not yet been able to push them back.

The one piece of good news is that Edinburgh still holds and whilst it does it will stand as a beacon of hope to humanity. The brave defenders have held off attack after attack whilst suffering enormous casualties from the heat-ray. Whilst the Martian effort is directed toward the taking of Edinburgh there is a good chance that we can reorganise our defences, produce and equip new armies and continue our technological advance in the hope of finding new weapons that will be effective against the Martian war machines.

The choices for us are difficult, it will take a large proportion of our resources to attempt to break the siege on Edinburgh which may leave us defenceless elsewhere. If we choose to abandon Edinburgh then the full weight of the Martian assault will fall upon the South of England and ultimately the Capital. It has already been shown that defence in itself, however strong, cannot contain the Martians but must be combined with offensive operations in order to succeed. Should we begin the assault now in the hope that the Martians have depleted their numbers despite their recent successes? Do we now have a chance to return to the offensive or should we wait until our numbers are greater and the chances of success are improved?

We know that if the Martians are left to themselves they will amass a new and more effective army and ultimately they will attack us with irresistible force. In the short term the key to the defence is Edinburgh and in the long term the key to success is the regaining of Liverpool and the Eastern coastal counties North of London.

Difficulty:

For the humans, this scenario will be a challenge. It will have it's surprises but ultimately you should succeed in defeating the Martians. Maintain your defences in Edinburgh and use them to whittle down his numbers. When you have lifted the siege use Edinburgh as a staging post taking the offensive to the enemy. The long Martian assault has left their lines of supply especially vulnerable to a foray from Edinburgh but beware as the Martians know your numbers are limited and will do everything they can to take Edinbugh. You have already lost a great deal of your manufacturing capability in the Midlands and you now have little in the way of resources. You may find it particularly difficult to keep the factories running. The Martians believe that they have you beaten and so it is just a matter of time before Edinburgh falls. Are they right? Can you relieve Edinburgh before it is lost to the Martian heat-ray?

For the Martians, this scenario is fairly easy due to the poor military and economic situation for the Human forces. Their lack of productive capability will be their downfall. The Humans are beaten but they have already shown that they will fight tooth and nail for every sector they defend. Regardless, the Martian losses will be sustainable and with the capture of Edinburgh they will have the benefit of greatly increased productive capability and protected lines of supply.

Dean Beedell 2005
