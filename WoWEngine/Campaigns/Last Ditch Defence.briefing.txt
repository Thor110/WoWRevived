The date is 1905

Summary:

The northern counties of Great Britain have been assaulted by the Martians and overrun. Liverpool has been attacked and taken. The undefended countryside beyond has been penetrated. The human defensive GHQ line has been assaulted time and time again whittling down the human defences to a shadow of their former capability.

Despite heroic efforts on the part of the human defenders the human defensive line known as the GHQ line, has been breached and overrun. The Martians are now pouring through the Western gap and are attempting to turn the flank of the GHQ line. It appears that the humans are no match against the Martian technologies and superior numbers. 

The Martian war machines have simultaneously assaulted all parts of the GHQ line but despite being thrust back in several key counties the heat ray has still wreaked havoc with the permanent human defences. The human armies in these counties have been wiped from the map. The human strategy to hold the Martian invasion has turned out to be a mistake. It has failed utterly and the assault appears to be unstoppable.

Human technological advance must continue in the vain hope of finding a weapon that will be effective against the Martians.

The heroic defence has lead to some difficult choices for the humans, it has been shown that defence, however strong, cannot contain the Martian's War machines by itself. Defence must be combined with offensive operations. A new direction is required and you have been appointed Commander in Chief of Her Majesties' Armed Forces in order to reverse the serious setbacks we have encountered so far. Due to the cost of the assault the Martians are now heavily depleted in numbers despite their recent success.

If the Martians are left to themselves they will amass a huge army and they will assault the vulnerable and unfortified areas to the West of the defensive chain. The key to the defence is now the regaining of Liverpool and the assault on the Eastern coastal counties North of London.

Difficulty:

For the humans, this scenario is very difficult indeed. You should rebuild your defences after assault, take the offensive successfully to the enemy. Use your defences to whittle down his numbers. Establish a forward offensive position and take the strategic offensive advantage away from the enemy. The long Martian assault has left their lines of supply vulnerable to attack. The Martians know this and will do everything they can to improve the chance of their next assault successding. You have very little in the way of resources but you must still keep the factories running. If you fail to do this you will lose despite the valiant efforts of your armies.

The Martians KNOW they have you beaten but can you turn the situation around? It seems impossible but it has been done - I doubt you will succeed. 

For the Martians, this scenario is surprisingly difficult despite the human's poor situation. The humans are beaten but they will fight tooth and nail for every sector. Martian losses will be high but they now have the benefit of enormous productive capability. They will win in the end.

Dean Beedell 2004
