Summary:

London has fallen and only the remnants of our forces remain. See how long you can hold out. Is it possible to win in this situation?

Difficulty:

For the humans, this scenario will be a great challenge. 

For the Martians, this scenario is easy due to the appalling military and economic situation for the Human forces. Their lack of productive capability will be their downfall. The Humans are beaten but they have already shown that they will fight tooth and nail for every sector they defend. The Martian losses will be trivial.

Dean Beedell 2005
