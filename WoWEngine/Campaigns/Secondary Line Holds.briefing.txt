Summary:

The northern counties have been successfully assaulted and overrun. The remnant of the human defences are stretched across the secondary defensive position.


The date is 1904

Despite heroic efforts to reinforce and defend their territories the human's defensive line North of Liverpool stretching across the Pennines to the Wolds has been overrun. The Martians have used their time well, enhancing their superior technologies and building sufficient numbers to realise their ambitions. Their war machines have marched across the Northen borders and burnt the human defences to the ground. The human strategy to check the Martian invasion has failed, they have only succeeded in delaying it.

The Martian strategy has concentrated on an advance to London itself and so whilst the lynchpin of the human defence is Liverpool the defence of London must not be neglected. 

Human technological advance has mainly been concentrated on the technologies of defence and when these have shown to be insufficient some technological progress has been redirected toward offensive capability that is also useful in defence.

The heroic defence has lead to some benefit for the humans, in that it has given them sufficient time to build and reinforce a second defensive line known as the GHQ line. 

The humans know that this time there will be no delay to the second Martian assault. The Martians are now amassing a huge army and they may assault any weak link in the defensive chain. The key to the defence is Liverpool which has been successfully defended despite all assaults against it and is now the bastion against the Martian invasion. If Liverpool falls the resources necessary to sustain the defence will be lost, the open countryside beyond will be open to Martian breakthrough. The Martians may attempt another frontal assault on Liverpool or they may choose to attack elsewhere. Their knowledge of the human dispositions will determine where. The sheer numbers of the Martian attackers must be seen to be believed. 

Difficulty:

For the humans this scenario is very difficult. You should rebuild your defences after the martian assault, take the offensive successfully to the enemy, destroying his bases in his front line positions.. Use your defences to whittle down his numbers. Establish a forward offensive position and take the strategic advantage away from the enemy. 

The long Martian assault has left their lines of support vulnerable to attack but the Martians know this and will do everything they can to improve the chance of their next assault. The Martians think they have you beaten but can you turn the situation around? 

I doubt you will succeed but it has been done. 

As the Martians, this scenario is surprisingly difficult, despite the advantages of large scale production finally coming online, the humans are still capable of fighting hard.

Dean Beedell 2004
