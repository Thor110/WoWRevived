The human defences are stretched across the northern counties. They have been successfully assaulted and overrun in one vital part by the Martian armies.

The date is 1899

The humans have set up a defensive line North of Liverpool from the Lake District stretching across the Pennines to the Wolds. They have attempted to fortify sufficiently to check the Martian invasion. Human technological advance has mainly been concentrated on the technologies of defence. This has lead to insufficient capability in the human offensive forces. Due to the lack of offensive actions against the martian forward bases the Martians have been able to launch an assault against the Western defences overrunning the human base in the Lake District. Liverpool is now vulnerable to assault and the Martians are building a base and preparing to do just that. There are currently no permanent human defences in Liverpool and only enough human troops to hold the line elsewhere. Luckily for the humans the Martian invasion force has spent itself on the assault giving them time to regroup.

Difficulty

As the humans this scenario is not a difficult one. You should be able to restore the balance, re-establish the line and take the offensive.

As the Martians, you have a good chance of winning this scenario if you can keep the pressure on Liverpool and prevent the humans from reinforcing. 

Dean Beedell 2004
