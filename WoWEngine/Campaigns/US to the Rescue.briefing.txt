Summary: The date is 1907

The human defences have failed and the Martian invasion has successfully conquered the whole of Great Britain. The British Empire is on the brink of total defeat. London is the last human stronghold in the British Isles and the remaining British forces have gathered there for the final stand. 

The Martians have swept down to the South Coast encircling London in an attempt to cut off all assistance from mainland Europe and to sever Britain from the remainder of British Imperial forces overseas, still stretched around the globe in an effort to maintain the Empire whilst preparing it for the Martian assault that is to come...

The Martians have made a major mistake in their strategy. They have focussed solely on conquering what they considered to be the dominant force on the planet, the British Empire. They have forgotten the strength of the emergent world power that is the United States of America. This federation of States has a significant industrial base that is only just beginning to overtake the industrial output of the United Kingdom. The US has never before needed to use it's significant industrial capacity in any major overseas military venture and has certainly never before envisaged sending an army to fight in Europe. Despite this it has rallied to the call recognising the threat that the Martians pose to the whole of humanity. The US has has sent large naval and military forces to the West coast of the United Kingdom to invade and liberate these isles from the Martian yoke.

The government of the US has wisely used the last few years of the conflict to advance it's technologies, equipping it's armies with the very best of weaponries available to man. The fleet has transported mobile manufacturing units to the coast and has succeeded in establishing a military bridgehead in four of the most Western counties of the UK. The small Martian armies occupying these areas were easily crushed and with growing confidence the US is preparing to push inland and liberate England from grip of the Martian invader. A single part of Scotland has also been liberated with the intent of pushing to the very heart and home of the Martians toward their stronghold in the Grampians. Ship-borne resources have poured into these areas in order to build the defences behind which the US forces are now able to shelter. These resources have now been consumed and these areas must now stand on their own two feet, seeking out and exploiting resources on the British Mainland for themselves.
 
Although the US forces are large and of the highest quality that man can create, the US may have seriously underestimated the Martian threat. The confidence of the US commanders will soon be shaken. The Martian armies have been enlarged for the forthcoming invasion of the continent and will be redirected to cope with this new threat. The Martians will not be so easily overcome. Their strength is so great that unbeknown to the Americans they are likely to be pushed back into the sea.

There is a choice for the US forces, to push eastward and liberate London, the capital of the Empire or push Northward to join with the US forces landing in Scotland. From this point they could just push onto the heart of the Martian stronghold. Both choices will have to take one critical factor into account, the paucity of human resources. The US forces must exploit every county that is liberated to it's utmost in order to restore the factories to a reasonable level of efficiency. Without these resources the liberation of the British Isles will founder and the Martians will succeed.

There is however, one truth that must be faced, if London falls then the worldwide resistance to the Martian invasion will fail and crumble. The world's hopes and fears lie with the hope that the resistance offered by the two most powerful nations on earth will be enough to overcome the evil aggression of the Martians. Losing London will end this hope and will cause the flight of all humanity before the relentless Martian war machine.


Difficulty:

As the humans this scenario is extremely difficult. You must NOT on any account let the Martians amass any army capable of assaulting London. As soon as any assault is launched on London the war is over. You MUST take the assault to the Martians and prevent them from ever launching this attack. you must exploit every resource to the maximum and suffer the low output of your factories until you control sufficient resources to start efficient production.

As the Martians you should not be able to lose but be doubly prepared as the human defencs are significant and you will have a surprisingly hard time overcoming them.

Dean Beedell 2005
