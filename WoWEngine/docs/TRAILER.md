# Trailer plan

The trailer is the last item in PLAN.md (G4). This file is the plan for it: what it shows, in what order, what each card
says and for how long, and how the footage is to be captured. It is written now so that the footage can be gathered as
the engine is finished; nothing here has been cut yet.

## What it has to do

- Show every feature the engine adds over the original game (MODERN.md), each long enough to be seen working.
- Compare the original's 640 x 480 with 1920 x 1080, side by side, on the screens where the difference is greatest.
- Say that the engine includes all of the features and file fixes made for WoWRevived, and show them.
- Mention WoWRevived's 1080p upscales of the movies, which the engine plays when they are installed.
- End with where to get it, that it needs the original discs, and the Discord.
- Be as long as it needs to be. The estimate below is about 4 minutes 30 seconds.

## Text on screen

Every card and caption stays up long enough to be read twice by a slow reader:

- **Duration = 1.5 s + 0.4 s per word, and never less than 3 s.** A five word caption is 3.5 s, rounded to 4 s; a
  fifteen word card is 7.5 s, rounded to 8 s.
- 0.5 s fade in and 0.5 s fade out, counted inside the duration.
- One card at a time, never more than two lines, never more than about 15 words. A longer point is split over two
  cards.
- Cards over footage sit in the lower third on a dark band, so the game's own panels stay readable above them.
- The game's own font is not used for cards: at 1080p it is too thin. A plain serif in the style of the menus' headings
  suits the period.
- The durations in the tables are the minimum for the text. Where the footage needs longer to show a feature working,
  the shot is longer and the card stays up for all of it.

## Capturing the footage

Every shot is recorded at 1920 x 1080. The engine runs 30 updates a second and draws at the display's rate.

- **The engine's frames headless.** `--screenshot` already saves a frame at any view size after a scripted run
  (HANDOFF.md has the recipes), and the comparisons' stills can come from it straight away: the 640 x 480 side with
  `--no-loose` and `[Screen] Size=640,480`, the 1080p side with `Size=1920,1080` and the engine's own `dat`. For moving
  footage, an engine option that saves every frame of a run (and the mixed sound as a WAV) would give clean video
  with no dropped frames, assembled with ffmpeg. With a playback recording (`--record`, then `--playback`) a scene
  can be played the same way as many times as it takes to get it right. This option is not built; it would be one
  update.
- **Screen capture.** Everything that is not the engine alone (WoWRevived's launcher and the original game, Windows /
  macOS / Linux windows, WoWLauncher installing from discs, Discord, the 1080p movies, real play with the sound) is
  recorded with OBS or similar on the owner's machines.
- **The battles.** The headless test battles have no enemy in range, so the fighting in section 7 is best recorded
  from real play, or from a campaign played to a point where the two sides meet, saved, and recorded from that save.
- **The original game.** For section 1 and the left halves of section 4, the original running at 640 x 480 (the
  original's own frames are better than the engine's `--no-loose` frames, which are meant to match them).

## Sound

The game's CD music and its movies are Jeff Wayne's and Sony's material, licensed to the game (the legal screen says
so); a trailer on YouTube with them in may be claimed by Content ID. To decide before cutting: the game's music with
the claim accepted, the game's sound effects and speech only, or music made for the trailer. The banner art is from
the album as well.
